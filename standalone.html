Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need to supply water or electricity? +

No! Our mobile van is 100% self-sufficient. We carry over 400 litres of spot-free deionised water and an ultra-quiet lithium inverter battery system. We can detail your car in an apartment carpark, driveway, or workplace.

How do I get my booking receipt and confirmation? +

Immediately after you complete the booking on this page, an official digital photo receipt with a unique HDB reference code is rendered. You can download the high-resolution photo, take a screenshot, and dispatch the receipt directly via Instagram to our team!

What areas across Brisbane do you service? +

We cover Greater Brisbane (CBD, Inner Suburbs, Northside, Southside), Western Suburbs (Indooroopilly, Kenmore), Springfield, Ipswich, Redbank, Logan, and surrounding areas.

What happens if it rains on the scheduled day? +

If you have a garage or undercover carport, we can proceed seamlessly. Otherwise, we will contact you in advance to reschedule to the earliest clear day at zero cancellation penalty.
